Location & Parking

DLo Esthetics is located in Honolulu, Oahu. Here's how to find us and where to park.

📍 Address

123 Kalākaua Ave, Suite 200
Honolulu, HI 96815

🕒 Studio Hours

MondayClosed
Tuesday – Friday9:00 AM – 5:30 PM
Saturday9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
SundayClosed

Parking Instructions

  • 1

    Studio Building Lot

    Enter the small lot on the side of the building off Kalākaua Ave. Metered stalls are available for clients — bring a card or coins for the meter.

  • 2

    Overflow Parking

    If the lot is full, the public parking garage one block away on Kūhiō Ave offers hourly rates and is about a 3-minute walk to the studio.

  • 3

    Street Parking

    Metered street parking is available along Kalākaua Ave; please check posted signs for time limits, especially during weekday mornings.

  • 4

    Arriving by TheBus

    Several TheBus routes stop within a block of the studio. Check TheBus trip planner for the route closest to you.

  • 5

    Checking In

    Once parked, head to Suite 200 and check in at the front desk. Please arrive 5–10 minutes early for your first visit.
