Location & Parking

D'Luxe Esthetics is located in the Kaimuki Professional Building on 12th Avenue, in the heart of Honolulu's Kaimuki business district. Here's how to find us and where to park.

📍 Address

Kaimuki Professional Building
1109 12th Ave
Honolulu, HI 96816

🕒 Studio Hours

MondayClosed

Saturday9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
SundayClosed

Parking Instructions

  • 1

    Building Lot

    Enter the lot at the Kaimuki Professional Building on 12th Ave. Metered stalls are available for patients and clients - bring a card or coins for the meter. While we do not validate parking, it is reasonably priced and costs $1/hr.

  • 2

    Street Parking

    Metered street parking is available along 12th Ave in the Kaimuki business district; please check posted signs for time limits, especially during weekday mornings.

  • 3

    Checking In

    Once parked, head into the Kaimuki Professional Building and check in at the front desk. Please arrive 5–10 minutes early for your first visit.
