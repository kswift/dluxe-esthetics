Before You Book

Booking, Cancellation & Appointment Policies

Your appointment time is reserved just for you. As a small, appointment-only studio, D’Luxe relies on advance notice. Last-minute cancellations and missed appointments leave an open slot that can’t be offered to another guest.

By booking an appointment, you acknowledge and agree to the policies below.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

  • 24+ hours’ notice: No fee.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: 50% of the scheduled service will be charged to the card on file.
  • No-shows: 100% of the scheduled service will be charged.

Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may require prepayment for future appointments.

Microneedling Appointments

Microneedling requires additional preparation and medical clearance. Before treatment, you must complete a consultation and receive clearance from our supervising Nurse Practitioner (NP). The NP consultation fee is separate from D’Luxe Esthetics and is paid directly to the NP.

Because microneedling appointments are longer and availability is limited:

  • 48+ hours’ notice is required to cancel or reschedule.
  • Less than 48 hours’ notice: 50% of the scheduled service will be charged.
  • No-shows: 100% of the scheduled service will be charged.

If your consultation and clearance aren’t complete by your appointment time, your treatment may need to be rescheduled, and the cancellation policy above will still apply.

Late Arrivals

Please arrive on time so you can enjoy your full service.

We offer a 15-minute grace period. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your service may be shortened or canceled, depending on the schedule and treatment. If your appointment must be canceled due to a late arrival, the applicable cancellation fee will apply.

Emergencies

We understand that life happens. Genuine emergencies will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Deposits & Card on File

A valid credit or debit card may be required to reserve your appointment. Your card will only be charged as outlined in these policies.

Any required deposit will be applied toward your service once it’s completed. Deposits are non-refundable but may be transferred to a rescheduled appointment when sufficient notice is given.

Refunds

If you have any concerns about your treatment or experience, please contact D’Luxe within 48 hours so we can talk it through and find the right resolution.
